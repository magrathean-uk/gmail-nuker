# Support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/magrathean-uk/gmail-nuker/issues) for reproducible bugs and focused feature requests. Include your commit, macOS/Python/Swift versions, sanitised command, expected result, and relevant error. Replace real mailbox addresses, subjects, targets, tokens, identifiers, credential paths, and private URLs with synthetic examples.

Start with [setup](docs/setup.md), [CLI reference](docs/cli.md), [operations](docs/operations.md), and [limitations](docs/limitations.md). Private vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Licensing, privacy, and sensitive support enquiries can go to [contact@magrathean.uk](mailto:contact@magrathean.uk), subject `gmail-nuker`.

Community support has no guaranteed response time or service level. The maintainer cannot recover permanently deleted mail, revoke your Google credentials, or access your mailbox on your behalf through this repository.

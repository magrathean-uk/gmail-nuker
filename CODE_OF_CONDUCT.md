# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors, users, and people discussed in issues with respect. Focus criticism on ideas and code. Welcome useful questions and different experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualised comments, impersonation, doxxing, publishing private correspondence or credentials, and deliberately misleading reports are unacceptable. Do not use project channels to coordinate spam, mass complaints, or harassment of senders or providers.

Maintainers may edit or remove material, close discussions, limit participation, or exclude participants when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and available evidence.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact [contact@magrathean.uk](mailto:contact@magrathean.uk), subject `CONDUCT: gmail-nuker`, initially sharing only enough information to establish a private route. Avoid public accusations and unnecessary personal data. The maintainer cannot guarantee absolute confidentiality or a fixed response time. Security reports belong under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
